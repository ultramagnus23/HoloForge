file. The cover letter does not name them.

21 October 2026, and the email is quoted exactly as that page writes it. None
is a collaborator, co-author or former supervisor of the author, who has no
co-authors on any paper.

## Do not suggest

- **Wechsler, Rizzo and Moser (EPFL).** Their concurrent work on differentiable
  wave-optical and photochemical optimization is discussed in the
  Introduction, so they have a competing interest.
- **Prof. John T. Sheridan**, who originated the NPDD model the paper builds
  on, **died on 1 November 2022** (obituaries from Optica and SPIE, and a
  University College Dublin memorial bursary in his name). He appeared as the
  first suggested reviewer in the JOSA A submission guide; that entry has been
  removed. Please do not reinstate it.

## Considered but not included

A fourth name would be welcome, but I could not verify a current institutional
email from an official page for any further candidate, and I will not supply an
address I have not seen in writing. Two worth looking up yourself if Prism asks
for a fourth:

- **Prof. Izabela Naydenova**, School of Physics, Clinical and Optometric
  Sciences, TU Dublin. Holographic photopolymer materials, including
  self-processing systems; the closest active specialist to this paper's
  subject. She is not among the cited authors, and the TU Dublin staff
  directory did not return an entry when searched, so the email needs checking
  on the Centre for Industrial and Engineering Optics page.
- **Dr. Friedrich-Karl Bruder**, Covestro Deutschland AG. Lead author of the
  Bayfol HX review whose Fig. 3 the recording model is fitted against. An
  industrial address rather than an institutional one, and he would be
  reviewing a paper that re-uses his published data, which is a reason to
  mention it to the editor rather than a disqualification.
- **Dr. Michael R. Gleeson**, co-author of two cited NPDD papers. His
  affiliation could not be confirmed past 2012, so no address is given here.
